01 — About

About me

I'm a computer science student at UC Berkeley who likes taking a project from a messy real-world problem to something people actually use. I've built a full-stack attendance system for a school of 600+ students and led a small team to ship a game on Steam — I like the parts of software that touch real users as much as the code itself.

Before Berkeley, I split my time between programming, orchestra, and a cystic fibrosis research project, so I tend to bring a fairly wide-angle, detail-oriented approach to whatever I'm building. Currently looking for internship opportunities where I can keep working across the stack.
